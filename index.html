// about

From hands-on engineering to engineering leadership

Over the past decade I have grown from hands-on engineering to Principal to Director, owning delivery for product teams and building distributed engineering functions across EMEA, APJ and North America.

I build engineering organisations where developers own outcomes end to end, feedback arrives at the point of change, and delivery decisions are led by data rather than opinion. My edge is making teams fast and reliable at the same time: I have owned delivery as a Principal Engineer running a product team, and now set engineering-wide delivery and quality strategy as a Director.

As organisations adopt AI-assisted development, I have led the work to make that shift safe and repeatable, using historical engineering data to define where and how AI-generated code can be trusted.
